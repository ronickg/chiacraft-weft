How it works

What the game does to draw the animal above, step by step, with its real data. Roll, breed, scar or tick Young and watch each step change.

  1. STEP 1

    Model

    Only boxes, each hung on a bone. Bones turn in animations; the bones the build genes scale are highlighted.

  2. STEP 2

    Painted texture

    Every box face painted once, by hand rules: light from above, hue-shifted shading, eyes stamped on. This is the "natural" look.

  3. STEP 3

    Coat layers

    Written next to the texture when the model is built: per texel, whether it is coat or fixed, its light, where on the body it sits, and where white, points, a mask, patterns and the iris may show. Genes repaint the coat using these, never the picture.

  4. STEP 4

    Genes

    One copy of each gene from each parent. The species' own genes pick its colors and patterns; the core traits every animal carries decide how this one wears them.

  5. STEP 5

    Look

    What the genes add up to: colors, pattern, how its marks are drawn, its eyes and scars.

  6. STEP 6

    Repainted coat

    The painted texture with its coat texels recolored from the look: the light from step 2 kept, the colors and marks new. Fixed parts (claws, teeth, nose) stay as painted.

  7. STEP 7

    Build

    The build genes scale single bones: a bone's own boxes grow or shrink, its children keep their size and move with its ends, and the animal is lifted so its feet stay down.

  8. STEP 8

    In the game

    Only the genome travels: 84 bytes per animal, saved with it and sent to everyone who sees it. Each player's game runs steps 4 to 7 itself.

Breeding lab

A population run by the game's own code (its Rust, compiled to WASM): matings, carrying, litters and clutches, eggs hatching, growing up, old age and families, on the game's clock.

Balance

Each species is a few real facts (its mass, how long it carries or hatches, grows up and lives, its litter, whether it lives alone, in a pack or a herd). The game's numbers follow from them by one pacing for all, so a new animal falls in line with the rest; a number a species sets itself shows “set”. The checks are the website tests’, against each rarity’s targets (a common pet’s line four generations deep in 10–25 in-game days, a legendary’s in 80–160). One in-game day is 20 minutes of play.

Evolution

One habitat's wild pool, a generation an in-game day: coats that stand out from the ground there leave fewer young, chance and mutation do the rest.
