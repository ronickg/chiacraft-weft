# chiacraft: the whole project, piece by piece

Written by `notes/overview/build.py` from `notes/overview/pieces.json`; edit that, then run the script. The visual board is `notes/overview/index.html`. How the gameplay systems connect: `notes/SYSTEMS.md`.

**The goal.** Players open their own worlds, find other people's without any central service, link them with portals and walk between them; missing mods download on the way; identity and achievements live on Chia. The game in those worlds: survival in the Valheim spirit with real temperatures and seasons, and animals to tame, breed and raise, from real facts.

- **Living world: Wind and fog, snow, storms**. Each biome's real place gives its mean wind and fog days per season (reports/ecology/Wind and fog.md); vitals::air makes each day calm or windy, foggy or not: fog before dawn burning off by mid morning, sea fog, ice fog, cleared by the wind. Rain falls as snow under 1 °C, snow lies and melts by degree-days, falling snow in a strong wind is a blizzard, dry ground lifts dust storms. The weather tells the engine (set_air: snow drawn where it is cold), the pack draws fog banks glowing toward the sun, blizzards, dust, snow on tops, swaying plants in gusts, slanting rain, rings on water, wet ground. Gameplay: wind chill out in the open (the wind chill index), fog shortens how far hunters and prey see. /air shows or sets it. Light shafts: a screen effect (mods/base/assets/shaders/post/shafts.wgsl) streaks the bright sky round the sun past what stands in front of it, strongest at low sun and in fog; the engine gives screen effects the scene. Snow lies as blocks near players (base:snow_layer, an eighth of a block a layer, 1 from a 0.2 cover to 4 when deep, plants buried from 2): a few random columns a tick near each player move a layer toward the cover, and each layer melts down on its own (a growth step, caught up when its land loads); far land keeps the pack's dusting (mods/base/src/snow.rs, vitals::air SNOW_*). In a forest the wind drops: deep under a closed canopy to a quarter of the open's, and its wind chill and blizzard with it, while the edge stays windy (vitals::air::shelter: the biome's canopy and how much of a ring 12 blocks round is under leaves). Each place's air is its own and its neighbours': temperature, humidity, seasons, wind, fog and snow lying blend over a 32-block band across a border, the snow layers too (base_api::around, vitals::air::AIR_BLEND). Still water freezes: near players the open top of tarns, lakes, ponds and the sea turns to lake ice under 0 °C (every visit from -6 °C) and thaws back as the snow melts, also away from players (ice's growth step, caught up on load); snow lies on the ice (vitals::air::freeze_chance, mods/base/src/snow.rs).
