# Overnight work, 2026-11-03

Everything is committed on `chihuahua-papillon-model`, one commit per piece, nothing pushed.
Screenshots: `notes/texture-test/screens/` (`show_1`: the new blocks in a wall; `land_1`, `land_2`:
fields, grass and flowers; `hud_final`: the HUD).

## Done

1. **Smooth text** (`0e1ba79`): Alegreya Sans, baked into a coverage atlas
   (`mods/base/source/ui/font_bake.py`), read by the engine (`Font::coverage`); lining numbers,
   wider spaces. Weft's pixel font stays the engine's own default.
2. **UI kit** (`f7e4e03`): the mockups' minimap ring, compass, quest panel and badge, key caps,
   portrait frame, dropdown, skill-tree nodes, map frame, zoom buttons, map markers and XP badge,
   kept in the kit for when those systems exist (the purple bar was dropped: it lost pixels on
   magenta; the kit's XP fill does the same job).
3. **Blocks at 64 px** (`1d48817`, `40bc9c2`, `b57210a`): 107 textures redrawn with Codex in one
   style, four per image: stones, bricks, sandstone, gravel, clay, the three woods (logs, planks,
   leaves), ores and their blocks, glowstone, obsidian, snow, ice, wool, farmland, grass and its
   side, crafting table, furnace, chest, plants and the eight wheat stages, lamps, pistons,
   observer, dispenser, ladder, door, the Cretaceous red sand, red sandstone and amber. Stone has
   three random variants, the oak planks lost their edge lines, the lit furnace got a new glow
   mask.
4. **Items** (`73c7953`, `3f15484`): every item icon in the new style (103): the kit's 42
   generated icons installed (they had never been put in the game), then food, all four armour
   tiers, buckets, torches, redstone parts, spawn eggs and the animals mod's items, four Codex
   sheets of 16. The sedative was redone on green (its violet vanished on magenta).
5. **New things with a purpose**
   - Paper (3 sugar cane), book (3 paper + leather), bookshelf (planks + books): a new kind of
     camp comfort, so more rested time. Sugar cane also makes sugar now (it had no recipe).
   - Hay bale (9 wheat, and back): storage, counts as something soft for comfort, and capybaras
     eat it (90 food points, nine wheat's worth).
   - Podzol: the spruce forests' ground, soil like dirt.
   - Dead bush: in deserts, breaking one gives 0 to 2 sticks (the desert's only wood).
   - Fried egg (furnace) and apple pie (apple, sugar, egg, wheat): two more foods for the
     well-fed buffs, from real nutrition data; `SHEET.md` regenerated.
   - Berry bushes (`e49fae9`): grow wild in forests, unripe, ripen through four stages; break one
     for a berry (three when ripe), plant berries for a new bush. Berries are a cooling fruit
     (vitamin C) and a capybara favourite.
   - Acacia (`86b0964`): the savanna's own tree, a new flat-topped shape (tall trunk, wide flat
     crown), with acacia logs, planks and leaves. It only grows wild for now (no sapling).
6. **HUD and screens**: an empty stomach is a faded food icon instead of a sentence, stack counts
   a size up with the smooth font, the dimming behind menus warm brown, the death screen a red-brown
   vignette instead of flat red.

## Not done, and why

- **Second font face** (Alegreya Bold for titles): the engine's text keeps one font per screen;
  a second needs the layout to carry a face per span (`text.rs`, `render.rs`). Worth an
  afternoon, not a night alone.
- **HUD layout like the mockup** (portrait top left, bigger hotbar): the portrait needs the
  player's face drawn into the HUD, which the engine can't do yet. The colours, font and counts
  are done.
- **Model-mapped textures** (torches, redstone dust, repeaters, comparators, levers, buttons,
  beds, the piston's inside): their models read exact pixel positions that a generated picture
  won't match; they need painting by hand or in code.
- **Glass, water and lava**: glass is see-through with soft edges (magenta can't give partial
  transparency), water and lava are 16-frame animations.

## Not yet seen in a real world

The acacia's flat crown and the wild berry bushes and dead bushes come from world generation, so
they show only in newly explored chunks of savanna, forest and desert; I checked the blocks by
placing them, not a generated savanna. Podzol likewise only in new taiga.
