# Nights by the moon (Ronald, 11-04: a full moon lights the night, no moon is dark, torches light
# the way). The moon lags the day by its phase (a turn every 8/7 day): day 0 of 8 full and up all
# night, day 3 a waning crescent before dawn, day 4 new (up by day only). Each case with nothing in
# hand, then a torch. Seed 1, --view 12, 1280x720, --shader-pack chiacraft, a fresh world.
# Creative on the ground, mobs off. Yaw 0 faces -z; the moon sets in the west (yaw 90).
loaded
idle

chat /weather clear 99999
chat /tk mode creative
chat /time set 6000

loaded
