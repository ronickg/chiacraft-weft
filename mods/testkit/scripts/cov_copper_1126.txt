# The copper tier's first steps (11-26, notes/COPPER.md; vitals::first::COPPER_LANDS). Seed 1, a
# fresh world.
# Part 1, worldgen: a census of native copper (base:loose_copper) within 64 blocks at the plains
# spawn (none expected: not a copper land) and in a taiga and a desert spot (taiga -96 -208 and desert -240 240,
# from land_find on seed 1). Expect 0 at the spawn, about 1-4 at each of the others (a find:
# `copper_lands_have_some` expects ~2 in a 64-block walk of open ground).
# Part 2, by real actions in survival on a pad: a lump picked up, put down on the ground, a plain
# strike with a rock (a knock: no flake, the lump stays), a sneaking strike opens the screen as
# "Hammering Copper"; the pick head picked, one strike inside the outline (a dent: the lump stays,
# the screen stays open), then the rest struck away: a copper pick head in the bag. Hafted from the
# book with a stick and two string: a pickaxe with a copper head. Then raw copper given: the
# copper_ore hint.
# Read: the /tk census replies, every /tk inv me and /tk get reply, run.log lines "first: strike"
# (copper true), "knapping: ... freed a copper pick_head", "first-clock: ... copper" and
# "copper_tool", "died" (none), and the shots (uc_screen: the copper title and how-to line;
# uc_hint_native, uc_hint_ore: the hints; uc_pick: the pick in hand).

where spawn

wait 1.7
