# Waterholes shrink in the dry season (`base`'s waterholes.rs, `vitals::climate::waterholes_shrink`):
# a pond's still water, a block deep, dries into `base:dried_mud` (on the snow's visits to columns
# near the player), receding from its corners down to one last block, and fills back when the
# season turns; thirsty deer then crowd round what is left. Biomes can't be set here, so
# `/waterholes dry 100` makes every place's still water shrink as in a dry season, 100 times as
# fast (a corner block dries on its first visit, about every 27 s); `wet 100` fills it back. Summer
# noon, so the pond doesn't freeze. The pond is right in front of the player (3-9 blocks north),
# looked down on steeply.
# Every 20 s while forced, a "waterholes: N water visits, M dried (not: ...)" line says what the
# visits found. PASS: the `count` replies for water in the pond fall from 49 to 1 (or near it by

# block stays); herd_waterhole_2.png shows a small pool in a bed of cracked mud with deer at it
# ("anim: <id> holds drink" after the water is set low); after `wet 100` water climbs back to 49
