# Does a herd keep itself fed through a savanna's winter (its dry season) on its own? The
# savanna's natural ground as worldgen made it, its tall grass and acacias untouched, but for a
# 9 x 9 rim round a 5 x 5 pond (water, as `herd_savanna_winter`: the deer's only sure water near).
# Five white-tailed deer, fed as they arrive (`animal::arrive`: 30 % plus the rest by how much
# their food grows here this season) and never topped up. One whole winter (14 game days) at 30
# times the game's pace (`/tick rate 600`: a game day in 40 s). Winter grass is poor fodder (a
# tuft feeds 1 point, 40 % growth), grazed tufts grow back slowly in the dry season, and a deer
# uses a point every 15 s (`SPECIES.md`: 120 food, 1.5 game days from full to empty), then
# lives 6 game days on its reserves before it starves (`needs::fasts`, by mass), so a deer that
# finds too little loses health slowly from about day 2-3 and dies from about day 7.
# Seed 1's savanna at x 0 z 544, as herd_savanna_winter.
# `/animalprof senses` each sample lists up to 8 animals within 48 blocks with their
# "food N% water N% health N".
# Not a pass/fail run: report, day by day (`/time query`), the deer's food and water from the
# senses rows, how many are still within 48 blocks, deaths (day, cause, where), the
# "holds graze" and "holds drink" lines a day, and the farthest any deer went from x 0 z 544.
# A herd that lives the winter on the savanna's grass, some thin, is what the ecology wants
# (`notes/ECOLOGY.md`, Capacity: "fewer and thinner" in the dry season); all dead within a few
# days says the grass or the fasting is too thin (`notes/ANIMALS.md`, the savanna runs).
# `/animalprof where` every 10 s (a quarter of a game day): every wild animal within 256 blocks,
# where it is and what it is doing, so a deer that leaves the pond shows how and when.
