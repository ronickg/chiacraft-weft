# Weather sound follows the weather and the cover (notes/SYSTEMS.md, weather sound).
# A stone floor high in the open sky with a closed 17x17 stone hut on it; the player stands
# outside, then inside, through clear, rain, a thunderstorm and clear again. Sound can't be heard
# in a script, so read run.log: `weather sound:` lines every 10 s (bed levels, `enclosed`, the
# weather the client has, the muffle), `thunder:` lines, and the `where` markers between steps.
# Expect: clear -> rain/heavy ~0 (wind only); rain outside -> rain up, enclosed ~0, muffle 20000;
# inside -> enclosed ~1, rain down to ~0.2 of outside, roof up, muffle under 1000 Hz;
# storm outside -> heavy up, `thunder:` lines; clear again -> beds fade, "all beds quiet" or wind only.
loaded
idle
chat /rule core:mob_spawning 0
chat /tk mode creative
chat /season summer 1
chat /time set 6000
chat /weather clear 99999
chat /tp 0.5 252 0.5
wait 2
loaded
chat /tk fill -40 249 -40 40 249 40 stone
chat /tk fill -40 250 -40 40 262 40 core:air
# The hut: walls and roof of stone, hollow inside (head 251.6, roof at 254).
chat /tk fill -9 250 -9 9 254 9 stone
chat /tk fill -8 250 -8 8 253 8 core:air
chat /tk fill -40 255 -40 40 262 40 core:air
wait 1
chat /tp 25.5 250 25.5
look 0 -10
wait 3
# ---- 1: clear, outside
where clear_out
chat /rain
wait 30
shot ws_clear_out.png
# ---- 2: rain, outside
chat /weather rain 99999
where rain_out
wait 45
shot ws_rain_out.png
# ---- 3: rain, inside the hut
chat /tp 0.5 250 0.5
where rain_in
wait 45
shot ws_rain_in.png
# ---- 4: back out in the rain (eases back up)
chat /tp 25.5 250 25.5
where rain_out2
wait 30
# ---- 5: a thunderstorm, outside
chat /weather thunder 99999
where storm_out
wait 60
shot ws_storm_out.png
# ---- 6: the storm from inside
chat /tp 0.5 250 0.5
where storm_in
wait 40
# ---- 7: clear again, outside
chat /tp 25.5 250 25.5
chat /weather clear 99999
where clear_out2
wait 45
shot ws_clear_out2.png
where end
quit
