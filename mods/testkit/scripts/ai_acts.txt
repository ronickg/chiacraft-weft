# The animals' act sounds (notes/crew/plans/ai-sound.md, "The acts"; animals_core::voices ACTS,
# eat_way, step_size): hungry grazers eat (animals:<kind>.eat), wolves come for them, the grazers
# bolt (animals:<kind>.alarm) and the wolves bite (animals:grey_wolf.attack); the heavy ones step
# (animals:<kind>.step: reindeer, protoceratops heavy, gastonia huge). A script can't hear: read
# run.log's `/sounds check` reply and "sound files:" line (no events missing files) and every
# `/sounds recent` reply. Expect in them: reindeer/protoceratops/gastonia/capybara .eat while they
# graze, then .alarm as the wolves close in, grey_wolf.attack on the bites, and .step lines if the
# list holds steps (the engine plays them on the client).
loaded
idle
chat /rule core:mob_spawning 0
chat /time set 6000
chat /weather clear 99999
chat /sounds check
chat /tk mode creative
chat /tp 0.5 206 0.5
wait 2
loaded
chat /tk fill -30 198 -30 30 198 30 stone
chat /tk fill -30 199 -30 30 199 30 base:grass
chat /tk fill -30 200 -30 30 212 30 core:air
chat /tk fill -30 200 -30 30 200 30 base:tall_grass
chat /tp 0.5 204 12.5
wait 1
loaded
# the grazers, hungry: they eat the grass
chat /tk spawn animals:reindeer -4 200 -4
chat /tk spawn animals:reindeer -2 200 -6
chat /tk spawn animals:reindeer 0 200 -4
chat /tk spawn animals:protoceratops 3 200 -5
chat /tk spawn animals:protoceratops 5 200 -3
chat /tk spawn animals:gastonia 8 200 -8
chat /tk spawn animals:capybara -7 200 -2
wait 2
chat /animalprof food 0.2
look 0 -20
wait 12
shot ai_acts_01_grazing.png
chat /sounds recent
wait 10
chat /sounds recent
# the wolves, hungry: the grazers bolt and the wolves bite
chat /tk spawn animals:grey_wolf 20 200 -20
chat /tk spawn animals:grey_wolf 22 200 -18
chat /tk spawn animals:grey_wolf 21 200 -22
wait 1
chat /animalprof hunters 0.2
wait 6
shot ai_acts_02_wolves.png
chat /sounds recent
wait 6
chat /sounds recent
wait 6
chat /sounds recent
wait 8
shot ai_acts_03_after.png
chat /sounds recent
quit
