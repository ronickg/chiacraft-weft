# A savanna's winter, as the climate has it (no `/waterholes dry` forcing): the savanna's dry
# season is its winter (`vitals::climate::dry_season`; the llanos' rain), so a pond's still water,
# a block deep, dries from its corners into `base:dried_mud` (`base`'s waterholes.rs) at its real
# pace, a corner block in about a game day (`climate::SHRINK_DAYS`), and the savanna's
# white-tailed deer drink at what is left on their own: at dawn and dusk from 80 % of their water
# (`routines::DRINKS_AT`), nothing set low. Spring fills it back (`climate::REFILL_SECS`).
# Seed 1's savanna at x 0 z 544 (`land_find -- 1 savanna`). The game runs at 30 times its pace
# (`/tick rate 600`: a game day in 40 s) so ten winter days pass in about 7 minutes; `/time query`
# after each sample says which day it reached (winter: its day modulo 56 is 42-55, the days
# counted from 0; if the PC can't hold 600 ticks a second, fewer). `/rain` says how often it rains
# here this season (winter low, spring high).
# `/waterholes watch` logs what the drying visits find every 400 ticks, under the real climate:
# "N water visits, M dried (not: ... edge, S season)": S must stay 0 all winter (the place is in
# its dry season) and be counted in spring.
# PASS: the `/time query` days stay in winter (42-55 of the 56-day year) until `/season spring`; the 5 x 5 pond's water count falls from 25 over the
# winter samples (water + mud = 25, water never 0), toward a last block or two by day 10;
# "anim: <id> holds drink" lines from the deer at dawn or dusk through the winter, and at the end
# some of them at the last pool (herd_savanna_winter_2.png: a small pool in cracked mud); in
# spring the water climbs back to 25 and mud to 0 (herd_savanna_winter_3.png). Report also how
# many days the samples reached (the real pace held) and any deer that left to drink elsewhere.

idle
