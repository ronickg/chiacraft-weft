# Day routines (reports/Creature AI.md; the crew's step 8): each species is about in its hours
# (Species::active). Red deer and hares are about at dawn and dusk (twilight). Flat pad by a pond,
# creative, still, mobs off; 12 red deer and 8 hares 6-18 blocks off, their water at 60%.
# Read the ANIMAL_SENSES lines at noon and at dusk: "about" (their activity: about 0.35 at noon,
# 1 at dusk) and "doing" (more rest at noon; at dusk they go to drink at 60% water, which at noon
# they leave until 50%), and the census line (resting / drinking).
loaded
idle

chat /tk mode creative
chat /air 0 0 0
chat /tk fill ~-30 ~-1 ~-30 ~30 ~-1 ~30 base:grass
chat /tk fill ~-30 ~ ~-30 ~30 ~3 ~30 core:air
chat /tk fill ~-3 ~-1 ~-14 ~3 ~-1 ~-10 base:water

chat /tk ring animals:red_deer 12 6 18
chat /tk ring animals:hare 8 6 14
wait 3
chat /animalprof water 0.6
wait 40
chat /animalprof senses
look 0 -20
wait 1
shot routine_noon.png

chat /animalprof water 0.6
wait 40

look 0 -20
wait 1
