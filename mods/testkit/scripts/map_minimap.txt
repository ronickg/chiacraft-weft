# The map (M) and the round minimap (HUD, top right), seen in the game (map_minimap.txt, 11-22).
# Part 1: a long flight over the land at the world's spawn (creative, flying, sprinting: the server
# marks the tiles round the player every second wherever they are, so the minimap fills along the
# way), the minimap shot at each of its zooms (N), a waystone woken, the full map at two zooms and
# a pan. Part 2: creatures on both, on a platform high in the sky: a tame (green), a zombie in
# sight (red), a cow and a deer in sight (pale), a zombie behind a stone wall and a sheep past
# the radius (neither shown); then the setting off (no dots).
