# Ambient life's sprites make their sounds from where they are (notes/SYSTEMS.md, ambient life):
# an eagle's cry from a circling bird, a gull call from the flock, an owl's hoot as it passes,
# splashes where a fish leaves the water and falls back. Seed 1, creative (spectators get no word
# from the server). Sound can't be heard in a script: read run.log's `ambient:` lines (every 10 s:
# what flies, then "sounds: N cries, N gull calls, N hoots, N splashes") and the `where` markers.
# Expect: eagles_and_gulls -> cries >= 1 over the 40 s (about one per 20 s) and gull calls in most
# lines (about two per 10 s); owl -> hoots >= 1 (one per crossing, the first crossing at once);
# fish -> splashes in every line, about twice the jumps; off -> no cries, gull calls or hoots
# (the sky floor's biome may still have its own). A cost check too: "ms a frame" stays under 0.05.
# Each line also gives the last sound's volume and muffle as the cover let it through: outside
# "volume 1.00, muffle 20000 Hz" (splashes leaving the water 0.45); in_hut (a closed stone hut,
# the gulls and eagles still overhead) -> about "volume 0.20, muffle 500 Hz" for the calls in it.
loaded
idle
chat /rule core:mob_spawning 0
chat /tk mode creative
chat /season summer 1
chat /weather clear 99999
chat /time set 6000
chat /tp 0.5 252 0.5
wait 2
loaded
chat /tk fill -40 249 -40 40 249 40 stone
chat /tk fill -40 250 -40 40 262 40 core:air
# a pool 21x21, one deep, sunk in the floor beside the player
chat /tk fill -10 248 -30 10 248 -10 stone
chat /tk fill -10 249 -30 10 249 -10 base:water
# a closed stone hut, 13x13 inside, roof at 254 (the cover's 7-block ring stays in its walls)
chat /tk fill 18 250 18 32 254 32 stone
chat /tk fill 19 250 19 31 253 31 core:air
chat /tp 0.5 250 0.5
wait 2
# ---- eagles on a thermal and a gull flock
chat /ambient force soar gull
where eagles_and_gulls
wait 42
# ---- an owl crossing (night)
chat /time set 18000
chat /ambient force owl
where owl
wait 42
# ---- fish jumping in the pool, many
chat /time set 6000
chat /ambient force fish busy
where fish
wait 32
# ---- the gulls and eagles heard from inside the hut
chat /ambient force soar gull
chat /tp 25.5 250 25.5
where in_hut
wait 32
chat /tp 0.5 250 0.5
# ---- back to what the place has
chat /ambient off
where off
wait 22
chat /ambient
where end
quit
