# The knowledge gate's first ruin (vitals::ruins, ruins.rs): the Stone ring near spawn, its
# pedestal giving the crucible blueprint once, and the crucible refused at a crafting table until
# the blueprint is read. One copy, a fresh world.
# Passes when every `until` meets. Read in run.log and the shots:
# - "nearest Stone ring ..." under 300 blocks from spawn (the first /ruin line);
# - ruin_above.png (12 above, looking down) and ruin_side.png (15 out): eight ley stone pillars
#   2-4 high (the broken ones moss-topped) round the edge of a level floor, the pedestal in the
#   middle; "pillars standing: 8 of 8" and a "pillar at x y z: n high (built n)" line for each;
#   ruin_pedestal.png: the pedestal close;
# - the second pedestal use gives nothing: "/tk slot me 1" is "- 0 0" and the /tk inv line holds
#   one blueprint_crucible;
# - ruin_locked.png: the crucible's bricks and coal in the grid, the result slot EMPTY;
# - ruin_unlocked.png: the same grid, the result a crucible.
loaded
idle
chat /ruin forget all
until chat blueprints known within 10
chat /ruin
until chat nearest within 10
# 1. The ring seen whole, in spectator (absolute places from /ruin goto): from 12 above, then
# from 15 out at eye height; and its pillars as they stand in the world, block by block.
chat /tk mode spectator
until mode spectator within 10
chat /ruin goto ring above
until chat beside the within 20
wait 3
loaded
idle
look 0 -60
wait 2
shot ruin_above.png
chat /ruin goto ring side
wait 3
loaded
idle
look 0 -5
wait 2
shot ruin_side.png
chat /ruin pillars
until chat pillars standing: 8 of 8 within 10
# 2. On its floor, two blocks south of the pedestal, facing it, in survival.
chat /ruin goto ring
wait 3
chat /tk mode survival
until mode survival within 10
loaded
