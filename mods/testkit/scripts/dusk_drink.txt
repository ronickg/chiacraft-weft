# Dusk drinking (creature AI step 8, fix a97f5992): a plant eater goes to water at dawn and dusk
# from 80% water (`routines::DRINKS_AT`), at other hours only under 50% (`needs::THIRSTY`). Before
# the fix the needs band still scored thirst from 50% at dusk, so they ate on instead (t2_routine8).
# The A/B: the same herd fed (100% food, so hunger doesn't take the turn: freshly spawned animals
# arrive hungry, which beat thirst in the first run, ai1122_dusk2) at 70% water, at noon and at
# dusk. The player is a spectator, whom animals don't sense (so curious ones don't come to look).
# PASS: at noon no ANIMAL_SENSES row says "doing drink"; at dusk most red deer and hares say
# "doing drink" (or "anim: <id> holds drink" lines after the dusk time is set), and dusk_drink_2.png
# shows them at the pond (straight ahead, 10-14 blocks off). Dusk is set to 17:30, still lit.
loaded
idle
chat /rule core:mob_spawning 0
chat /tk mode creative
chat /air 0 0 0
chat /tk fill ~-30 ~-1 ~-30 ~30 ~-1 ~30 base:grass
chat /tk fill ~-30 ~ ~-30 ~30 ~3 ~30 core:air
chat /tk fill ~-3 ~-1 ~-14 ~3 ~-1 ~-10 base:water
wait 2
chat /tk time 6000
chat /tk mode spectator
chat /tk ring animals:red_deer 12 6 18
chat /tk ring animals:hare 8 6 14
wait 3
chat /anim log
# Noon (hour 12): 70% water is above THIRSTY, so nobody drinks.
chat /animalprof food 1.0
chat /animalprof water 0.7
wait 15
chat /animalprof senses
wait 15
chat /animalprof senses
look 0 -20
wait 1
shot dusk_drink_noon.png
# Dusk (hour 17:30, the DUSK window 17-20): 70% is under DRINKS_AT, so they go to the pond.
chat /tk time 11500
chat /animalprof food 1.0
chat /animalprof water 0.7
wait 15
chat /animalprof senses
look 0 -20
wait 1
shot dusk_drink_1.png
wait 15
chat /animalprof senses
look 0 -20
wait 1
shot dusk_drink_2.png
quit
