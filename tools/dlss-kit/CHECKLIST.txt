chiacraft DLSS on Windows: the test (about 30 minutes, most of it unattended)
============================================================================

BEFORE, on CachyOS (2 minutes)
  1. Terminal:  ~/win/chiacraft-dlss-kit/copy-to-windows.sh
     (asks your password once to mount the "Storage" drive, copies the kit, ~2.5 GB)
  2. Reboot into Windows.

ON WINDOWS
  3. Driver: any recent Game Ready driver runs DLSS and Ray Reconstruction. (DLSS 5 will need
     616.64 or newer, later.)
  4. Explorer > Storage drive > chiacraft-dlss-kit. Click the address bar, type  powershell , Enter.
  5. Quick check (~4 minutes; the game window opens and closes by itself three times):
        powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\run_kit.ps1 -Quick
     Open results\<newest>\summary.txt. Good when:
       - no-streamline : says core:upscaler 1 (dlaa) is off  (the game starts without NVIDIA's files)
       - dlaa          : says "streamline: DLSS ... ready" and "upscaler dlaa: world 2560x1440"
     If dlaa says failed / missing / not supported: run the same line with  -Dev  added and keep
     chiacraft\streamline-dev\sl.log. Skip to step 8.
     Smaller screen than 2560x1440? Add  -Size 1920x1080  to each line.
  6. Full run (~25 minutes, you can leave it):
        powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\run_kit.ps1
  7. Look, 2 minutes: in results\<newest>\ compare  spotc_off\vistac_h08.png  with  spotc_dlaa,
     spotc_sr-quality,  spotc_sr-quality-cnn  (the older model)  and  spotc_rr  (same file), and the
     *_moving.png  shots (taken mid-strafe).
     Note in a few words: sharper or softer than off? shimmer? ghost trails behind leaves? black or
     broken frames? Which looks best?
     And one word: is  sr-jitterflip  worse (speckled canopy) than  sr-quality?  (yes / no / same)

AFTER, back on CachyOS
  8. Terminal:  ~/win/chiacraft-dlss-kit/fetch-results.sh   then tell Planner "DLSS results in"
     with your notes from step 7. (Frame times are in summary.txt: BENCH lines, "frame avg".)

GOOD TO KNOW
  - DLSS 5 rows are skipped: NVIDIA has not published its plugin (sl.dlss_nr) in the public SDK.
    To get it, sign in at developer.nvidia.com and ask for DLSS 5 / Neural Rendering access.
  - Ray Reconstruction gets approximate inputs for now (normals from depth, albedo from the picture).
  - In the game: Options > Video and Quality > the DLSS tab: mode, model per mode, Ray Reconstruction
    and its model, all live.
  - Nothing here changes Windows or the driver. The game writes only inside this folder
    (chiacraft\run, results\). A run that hangs is stopped after 5 minutes.
